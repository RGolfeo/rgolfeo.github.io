Prospect sourcing SOP

The rules I follow so every batch is clean, verified, and ready for outreach on day one.

Lead generationHealthcareRepeatable

1. Target

  • Agree on the region, roles, and license types before sourcing starts.
  • Prioritize independent practitioners, new practice owners, and recently licensed clinicians.
  • Exclude retired, inactive, and admin-only contacts.

2. Verify

  • Confirm recent license issuance and current clinical activity with reputable sources.
  • Treat a historical license as proof of nothing current.
  • Flag associate licenses and anything uncertain instead of guessing.

3. Email rules

  • Publicly posted, individual professional emails only.
  • No shared info, admin, or reception inboxes.
  • Never guess an address or use people-search databases.
  • Never claim an email is deliverable without verifying it.

4. Deduplicate

  • No repeats inside a batch or against earlier batches, and any limits on that check are stated.

5. Deliver

A fixed count per batch (20 by default) in a copy-ready format:
