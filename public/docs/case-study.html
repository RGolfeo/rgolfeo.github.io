The problem

The team needed qualified practice contacts, organized prospect tracking, and steady LinkedIn and email outreach without duplicate leads or lost follow-ups.

What I did

  1. Source: Sales Navigator and professional directories, filtered by role, specialty, and location.
  2. Verify: confirm the role, the practice, and recent licensing from reputable sources.
  3. Score: tier every prospect by fit (149 Tier A and 78 Tier B in one sourcing batch).
  4. Reach out: personalized LinkedIn requests and professional email outreach.
  5. Track: every record carries a status and a next action.
  6. Follow up and report: scheduled nudges plus daily start and end updates.

The result

One operating view now covers 287 tracked LinkedIn leads, 170 outreach-ready prospects, and 57 high-priority opportunities, with status and follow-up visible to the whole team.
