Daily reporting template

A start and end-of-shift update a client can read in under a minute, converted to their time zone.

Async updatesClient-readyCopy and paste

Start of shift

Hi [Client Name], Starting my shift now: [Start time, client time zone]. Today's focus: 1. [Priority task] 2. [Second task] 3. [Follow-ups due] Best, Ryan

End of shift

Hi [Client Name], Ending my shift: [End time, client time zone]. Completed: - [Prospects sourced / verified: #] - [Outreach sent: # LinkedIn, # email] - [Replies and next steps] In progress: [Task + status] Blockers: [None / what I need] Tomorrow: [Top priority] Best, Ryan

Why it works

  • Same structure every day, so it is easy to scan.
  • Numbers first, then what needs the client's attention.
  • Times shown in the client's time zone, not mine.

Sample shared by Ryan Golfeo. Client names and identifying details are removed.
