Credentialing support workflow

How I support provider credentialing: status checks, document verification, tracking-log updates, and payer follow-ups.

The workflow

  1. Intake: capture the provider, practice, payer, and what is pending on a simple notes template.
  2. Verify: check NPI and practice details in the NPI Registry, and employment dates in CAQH ProView.
  3. License lookup: CAQH state license first, then the state verification portal, then the medical board.
  4. Documents: confirm applications, EFT documents, and supporting files are complete.
  5. Track: update the tracking log with status, date, and next action.
  6. Follow up: payer and provider emails until each item is resolved.

Intake notes template

Provider: [Name, credentials] Practice: [Name] | NPI: [#] Payer: [Name] | Application status: [Status] Missing items: [List] Next action: [What + by when] Notes: [Anything unusual]
